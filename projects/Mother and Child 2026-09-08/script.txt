A child is not a limitation on your freedom.
It makes you someone in particular.

I chose particular.
Actual names. Actual days. Actual hands.

Today I saw a pregnant woman on a bus.
No one gave her a seat.
Someone behind her called her a slut.

That is a polis that owes nothing forward,
so it carries nothing forward.
Not even a seat.

I chose particular.
That is the tradition. I am not theorizing it.
I am running it.
